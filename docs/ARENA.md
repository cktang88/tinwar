# Arena hazards and props

The arena itself has surprises in Free for All, Team Deathmatch and Domination (`src/shared/sim/barrels.ts`, `src/shared/sim/airdrop.ts`, drawn by `src/client/arenafx.ts`):

- **Explosive barrels.** Rust-red barrels stand in clusters of two or three on every versus map (a `b` in the map grid; they turn about the centre like crates, and `node scripts/map-lint.ts` fails a barrel in a wall, on a crate, over a zone, within 120 px of a spawn region or without its half-turn twin). Bullets stop on them and wear them down; a hurt barrel leaks smoke and sparks, and at zero it hisses and flashes for under half a second, then bursts as a blast of 170 px. A barrel caught in a blast lights a shorter fuse the nearer it was, so a row goes off one after another. A barrel kill credits whoever lit the barrel (teammates are spared in team modes, and your own barrel still hurts you) and earns **Kaboom**; a chain of two or more barrels that kills two or more players earns its igniter **Chain Reaction**. A burst barrel leaves a scorch and stands again after 45 seconds, though never under a body.
- **Airdrops.** Once or twice a round, at a random time (fixed by the round's seed, so a replay is exact), the kill feed and minimap announce "Supply drop inbound". A cargo plane's shadow sweeps across the map along a dashed track on the minimap, cuts a crate loose over a random reachable spot, and it floats down on an orange and bone parachute for five seconds. The landed crate wears orange beacon brackets, a SUPPLY DROP plate saying what it gives, and a minimap marker; break it by shooting it (or a blast) within 75 seconds. Whoever breaks it **skips straight to their next level pick** (the next attachment, gun evolution, perk or ability) and gets a full resupply on top: full health and armor, a full magazine and the ability back. Someone with every pick already made gets a **golden gun** instead, which hits 20% harder and gleams for the rest of that life. The plate over the crate names the next pick of whoever is looking. Either way it is a **Special Delivery**.
- New medals: Kaboom (bronze, 30), Chain Reaction (gold, 100), Special Delivery (silver, 50).
- **Props.** Beside the barrels stand eight more things to shoot (`src/shared/sim/props.ts`, tuned in `PROPS` and `PROP_FX` in `src/shared/defs.ts`, drawn by `src/client/propfx.ts`; map symbols `p g e o l m a i`, which turn about the centre like the barrels and are checked by `node scripts/map-lint.ts` for walls, crates, barrels, zones, spawns within 100 px and a twin of the same kind). Every prop stops bodies and rounds while it stands, wears down under fire (dents, then leaks) and, like a barrel, credits whoever set it off; a blast sets props off too, so a barrel chain can start any of them. They go away for a while and stand again, never under a body, and they exist only on the versus maps (Zombies and Last Standing have none).
  - **Propane tank.** Cream with an orange valve. At zero it vents and **rockets off along the heading of the round that hit it** (away from a blast), tumbling and trailing a flame, and skids with its speed bleeding off until it touches a wall, a crate, a body or another prop, or runs out of its 1.8 seconds, then bursts as a blast of 120 px and up to 95 damage. The kill is a **Liftoff**.
  - **Gas canister.** Olive with a toxic band. Bursts into the gas grenade's cloud (140 px, 14 damage a second dealt as a pulse of 7 every half second, lasting 7 seconds): area denial. The one who shot it is spared, as with the grenade.
  - **Generator** (electrical box). Gunmetal with hazard stripes. Shot to zero it arcs for 0.6 seconds, then pulses an **EMP**: everyone within 210 px (friend or foe) is slowed to 55% for 2.6 seconds and cannot use an ability for 2 seconds, shocked players crackle with arcs, and lamps in reach go dark for a couple of seconds. Killing a player it has shocked, while you are the one who shorted it, is **Shock Therapy**.
  - **Oil drum.** Dark gunmetal with an amber drop. Spills a **burning slick** (100 px, 18 damage a second dealt as a pulse of 9 every half second, 6 seconds) that burns anyone in it but its spiller and glows as a light source. A kill by fire is an **Arsonist**.
  - **Streetlamp.** A tall post with a lamp head and a pool of light. Shoot the bulb and the pool goes dark and the stump sparks; the post stays and blocks, and the bulb comes back 90 seconds later. The simulation tracks only whether it is broken, so everyone sees the same lamp; the light itself is the client's.
  - **Medical cabinet** and **ammo crate.** Walk up to one when you need what it holds (hurt, for a medical cabinet; a magazine not full or your ability cooling, for an ammo crate) and it opens by itself and hands you its pack at once: there is no key, your body coming within 70 px of its centre (pressed to any side or corner, with room to spare) is enough, and the first player in the tick's turn order who needs it gets it. Walk past it at full health (or full of rounds with the ability ready) and it stays shut for whoever needs it. Shoot one open instead and it shatters and leaves a pack (a mint ring, or an amber one) lying 25 seconds. Walk over the health pack when hurt for 50 health, or the ammo pack for a full magazine and your ability ready at once: there is no key, your body brushing the pack takes it. Neither is wasted: a pack is not taken by a player at full health (or with a full magazine and the ability ready), and stays for whoever needs it. Whatever you take pops up over your own soldier, only on your screen, as small toy chips that rise and fade: a mint cross with the health it actually gave ("+12" when you were 12 short), an amber round with the rounds it put back, a spark bolt and READY for the ability, and the same for a supply drop (a gold star for a golden gun). Pickups at the same moment share a row, and a later one lifts the older rows. Bots play by the same rules: one hurt or short of rounds with nobody to fight walks to the nearest pack it needs, or up to a cabinet, which opens for it as for a person (`src/server/bot/supplies.ts`); the client side is `src/client/pickups.ts`.
  - **Paint can.** Shot, it splatters your colour on the floor (a decal that fades over a minute and a half, hurting nobody); a splatter that lands over an enemy is a **Picasso**.
  - Bots path round solid props through the nav grid like barrels, shoot a gas canister, oil drum, generator or a propane tank lined up on an enemy when it would reach one and none of their own, hold fire down a line that meets a hazard beside them, and keep out of fire and gas: out of one they stand in, held at the edge of one on their way.
  - New medals: Liftoff (silver, 50), Shock Therapy (silver, 50), Arsonist (bronze, 40), Picasso (bronze, 25).

Every medal a kill earns is paid and announced together, so one sniper kill from across the map can land Long Shot, One Shot and Eagle Eye at once, each toast lining up beside the last. The weapon medals judge the gun whose round landed the kill.
